# Feature Specification

### Alumni
Registration, login, editable own profile, paginated alumni directory, and search/filtering. Alumni directory results omit private contact details.

### Events
Faculty and administrators can create events; faculty manage their own and administrators approve pending events. Alumni can view approved events, register, and cancel their own registrations.

### Careers
Alumni can create, edit, and delete their own job postings. Students can record an in-app application and separately open a validated employer application URL.

### Mentorship
Students can discover mentors, request mentorship, and cancel pending requests. Alumni can accept or reject pending requests and complete accepted relationships. Faculty can view department-level totals.

### Communication
Faculty and administrators can publish announcements. Creators and administrators can edit or delete announcements. Notifications are in-app records, not email delivery.

### Donations
Alumni can record donation pledges and view their history. Administrators can review records and mark pending records completed or failed. This is recordkeeping, not payment processing.

### Administration
Dashboard with alumni count, events, registrations, jobs, mentorship,
and donation statistics.
